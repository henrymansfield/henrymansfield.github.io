Past research

► Extracting Carbon Nanotubes from Plastic Waste Catalysis Byproducts
Status: Complete  |  Tools: Controlled Vacuum Filtration, Scanning Electron Microscopy, Thermogravimetric Analaysis, Raman Spectroscopy, UV-VIS Spectroscopy, Centrifugation, Annealing

Designed purification processes to extract usable carbon nanotubes (CNTs) from byproducts of Cecilia Energy's reactors. I also experimented with acid washing and annealing. I performed extensive characterization on the films and solutions I made. Done with Jacques Doumani.

💻 Projects

► Wearable PCB
Status: In Progress  |  Tools: KiCad, Simplicity Studio, Bluetooth Low Energy

Small PCB for interfacing with flexible sweat based biosensors, reading sensitive analog data, and transmitting it to an app over bluetooth low energy. Designed and programmed with Emmanuel Zerefa.

► Embedded System Beat Saber
Status: In Progress  |  Tools: MSP430 MCU, general embedded system design/programming

Recreated popular game beat saber in embedded system form. Visuals on LED strips, powered from wall outlet, controlled with accelerometers and MSP430 microcontroller. Designed and programmed with Emmanuel Zerefa and Ben Welchman.
